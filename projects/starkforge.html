Project Overview

Abstract concepts in computer science—such as digital logic gates, binary arithmetic, and TCP three-way handshakes—are often taught purely through static textbook diagrams. Without immediate interactive feedback, students struggle to build intuitive mental models of how these systems react.

StarkForge addresses this challenge with zero-dependency browser simulations. By manipulating interactive inputs, wiring virtual logic gates, and tracing packet sequences step-by-step, learners can experiment with foundational concepts directly in the DOM.
